# Character Bible — Get Outside Mid-Atlantic · Ops Center

**Status:** Locked visual + voice reference for Grok Imagine, social, and site art.
**Style:** Construction-paper / flat cutout, bold outlines, limited palette, deadpan faces. South Park–adjacent energy without copying any IP. Original characters only.
**Do not use:** Riley (retired name). SOC as user-facing brand (Ops Center only).

---

## The unit

They are the **Ops Center field team** — the faces of “what’s worth getting outside for this weekend” across the Mid-Atlantic. Not influencers. Not mascots with slogans. Field operators who show up, read the map, and slightly misjudge the logistics.

Tone across the cast:
- Deadpan, practical, lightly competitive with each other
- Never corporate cheer
- Never mean; mild chaos is allowed
- Region pride without state-war toxicity

Shared visual rules:
- Flat paper shapes, hard edges, minimal gradients
- Big simple eyes, small mouth, underreacting expression
- One clear prop per shot (map, paddle, beanie, etc.)
- Outdoor Mid-Atlantic setting always readable in 1 second (water, fairgrounds, hills, boards, orchards)

---

## Dan — Ops lead / map person

**Role:** Unofficial team lead. Carries the plan. Also the one who updates the plan three times.

**Look**
- Neutral outdoor layers (earth tones, soft greens, muted blues)
- Often holding a folded paper map or phone with a route
- Calm, slightly furrowed brow; “I already know this will go sideways”

**Personality**
- Prepared. Over-prepared. Still surprised by parking.
- Speaks in short operational lines: times, distances, weather windows
- Soft authority — the others defer, then do their own thing anyway

**Signature beats**
- Maryland Ag Fair with the map open against a barn or midway
- Delaware peach-run / orchard stop, still checking the itinerary
- “We’re fine. We’re five minutes behind. We’re fine.”

**Do**
- Center of composition when the shot is “the plan”
- Map or checklist as prop

**Don’t**
- Hero pose, cape energy, or motivational poster face

---

## Jax — water / river person

**Role:** Paddle sports, river festivals, anything that involves getting wet on purpose.

**Look**
- Sportier silhouette: vest, quick-dry, sun-faded colors
- Paddle almost always in frame
- Slightly more alert expression than Dan; still deadpan

**Personality**
- Optimistic about conditions
- Underestimates how cold the water is
- Quietly competitive about who “found” the better put-in

**Signature beats**
- Virginia Riverfest, paddle on shoulder, festival banners soft in background
- Kayak/canoe edge-of-frame, not product-shot clean

**Do**
- River, dock, or festival-by-water as setting
- One paddle as the readable prop

**Don’t**
- Extreme sports hero framing or big splash action stills

---

## Sam — hills / folk / inland person

**Role:** Mountains, trails, small-town festivals, music under tents.

**Look**
- Beanie or soft hat, layered flannel or canvas jacket
- Earth palette (browns, olive, rust)
- Softest expression of the four; still not smiling big

**Personality**
- Low-key. Notices the band, the food line, the ridge line.
- Least likely to rush; most likely to say “we should just stay here”
- Dry one-liners about parking and “scenic routes” that weren’t

**Signature beats**
- Pennsylvania folk fest / small-town fair energy
- Beanie + paper cup or instrument silhouette optional, not required

**Do**
- Inland, festival, or hill-country backdrop
- Beanie as the instant read

**Don’t**
- Hipster parody or heavy facial hair detail (keep cutout-simple)

---

## Kevin — chaos / fairgrounds person

**Role:** State fairs, midways, “this seemed like a good idea” logistics.

**Look**
- Slightly messier silhouette than the others
- Broken or duct-taped paddle, tilted hat, or fair snack in hand
- Same deadpan face while something minor has clearly gone wrong

**Personality**
- Enthusiastic starter, imperfect finisher
- Owns the mishap without a speech
- The reason the group has a story later

**Signature beats**
- West Virginia State Fair, broken paddle, midway lights or tents behind
- Still standing in line. Still committed.

**Do**
- One clear “something went mildly wrong” prop
- Fairground or carnival structure readable in background

**Don’t**
- Actual injury, danger, or mean-spirited failure
- Slapstick face — keep the deadpan

## Ensemble rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Cast size | Lead with one character per state card unless the prompt is explicitly “team shot” |
| Team shot | Dan slightly forward or center; others flanking with their props |
| Text | Minimal. Prefer expression + prop over dialogue bubbles |
| Branding | “Ops Center” only in UI/chrome — not printed on character clothing unless asked |
| Continuity | Same face shapes and palette across weeks so the cast is recognizable at thumbnail size |
