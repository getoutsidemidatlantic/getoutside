## Pick #1: Cape Henlopen Sunset Kayak Tour

**Region:** Delaware
**Date/Time:** Weekend evenings; check tour schedule
**Location:** Cape Henlopen State Park, Lewes, DE
**Category:** Boating/Paddling
**Cost:** Paid guided tour; rentals also available
**Best For:** Beginners, couples, nature lovers
**Source:** [Cape Henlopen State Park — Delaware State Parks](https://www.destateparks.com/park/cape-henlopen/)

**Description:**
A no-experience-needed sunset paddle on the Delaware Bay side of the park, launching from the fishing pier. Dolphins, osprey, rays, and skates are common summer sightings, with lighthouse views the whole way.

**Logistics:**
- **Parking:** Park entrance lot; follow signs to the fishing pier
- **Distance:** ~2 hours from Philadelphia
- **Contact:** Check destateparks.com for tour times

**Social Post Fit:** Instagram, TikTok
